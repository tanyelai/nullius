## Required sections

One per line, walked in full every time. Syntax:

    - Name | an alias it may appear under | another alias | min 120

`min` is the word count below which a section is reported as thin. It is a threshold you chose,
so it reports and never blocks. Aliases matter: a heading reading "Limitations and Future Work"
should satisfy an entry named "Limitations".
