# Scholarship

**Search the field's vocabulary, not your own.** An entire literature can sit one synonym
away. Log each vocabulary separately, because one query and forty look identical in prose.

**Silence is a failed search, not novelty.** An empty neighbour set almost always means the
terms were wrong.

**Corroboration requires independence.** Two papers sharing an author are one source wearing
two citekeys; five citing each other are an echo.

**A survey's description of a paper is not the paper.** It is an `authors-claim` about it.

**The abstract is a marketing document.** So is yours. What a study shows is in its tables.

**Your own unpublished data gets a published paper's bar.** `n=8` is `single-result` whoever
ran it.

**Say what you do not understand.** An empty list of what remains unaccounted for is itself a
claim, and usually a false one. That list is `nullius needs`.

**Retracted and preprint are facts on the record.** Let them travel with the citation.

**Never fabricate an identifier.** Resolve it with `nullius cite`, or say you could not.
