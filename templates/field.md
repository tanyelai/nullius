# Field

What is normal here. This file is what makes a critique calibrated rather than idealised --
a finding that cannot name the norm it violates is taste, and this is where the norms live.

## What counts as evidence

Which claims need what. Where a simulation is acceptable and where it is not. What has to be
measured on held-out data, and what the field will accept in-sample.

## Normal scale

Typical sample size, and the number below which reviewers object. Typical number of seeds,
sites, subjects, datasets.

## Standard baselines

What every paper is expected to compare against, and what it means when one is missing.

## Standard reporting

The checklist or reporting standard this field uses, if it has one. What must be stated about
the data, the split, the metric, the variance.

## Known traps

Where this field's results usually fail to transfer. Contamination, leakage, selection,
distribution shift, the benchmark everyone knows is saturated.
